# Enterprise Cybersecurity GRC Toolkit & Audit Frameworks

Welcome to my professional cybersecurity knowledge repository. This space hosts practical, field-tested Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) frameworks, IT audit work programs, and strategic security policies. 

Rather than focusing on software development, this repository serves as a living portfolio of structural cybersecurity methodologies designed for enterprise environments, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.

---

## Profile & Core Expertise
I am a cybersecurity professional and IT Auditor specializing in IT Governance, Risk Management (GRC), and infrastructure control verification. My focus is translating deep technical vulnerabilities into measurable business risks for executive-level decision-making.

* **Core Focus:** Information Security Policies, IT General Controls (ITGC) Audits, Risk Assessments, and Regulatory Compliance.
